What Aserta does in Shopify

Continuous product page monitoring

Product pages evolve over time. Every update has the potential to introduce compliance drift. Aserta automatically re-audits monitored product descriptions whenever they're updated in Shopify, helping you identify issues before they spread across your catalogue.

* Automatic Allergen Emphasis

UK Food: detects all 14 major allergens and emphasises them in ingredient lists (FIR-style bolding). Supports Contains: lines and common ingredient declaration formats.

Per product audit trail

Every audit records a compliance status in Shopify metafields, allowing your team to see Compliant, Needs Review, or detailed findings for every monitored product after each audit.

Print ready labels

Generate print-ready labels directly from audited product data, helping reduce repetitive manual work while keeping your product information consistent. You remain responsible for confirming that printed labels accurately reflect your approved legal copy.
