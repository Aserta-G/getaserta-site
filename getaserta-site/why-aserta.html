Your labels get reviewed. Your Shopify copy keeps changing.

Most compliance gaps we see are not ignorance — they are catalogue drift. A new variant launches, UK copy is pasted into a UK listing, or a bundle SKU never gets a full ingredients block. Aserta re-scans Shopify product descriptions when products change — so gaps surface before they spread across your catalogue.

Automated (Not AI) assistance on Shopify text — not legal advice, not physical label sign-off.

Monitor. Apply. Review.

Monitor: Continuous Product Page Checks

Product pages change. Compliance shouldn't depend on someone remembering to check them.

Whenever a monitored product description is updated in Shopify, Aserta automatically re-runs the compliance modules you've enabled. Every product receives a compliance status stored in Shopify metafields, including Compliant, Needs Review, or a detailedfindings trail.

Instead of manually revisiting hundreds of product pages after every catalogue update, your team can immediately see which products require attention.

Apply: Where the rules are clear

Where regulatory requirements are objective and well defined, Aserta applies consistent formatting automatically.

UK Food (Available Today): Ingredient lists are scanned for the 14 major allergens and, where appropriate, allergen names are automatically emphasised using FIR-style bold formatting. Every subsequent edit is re-checked to help prevent compliance drift.

As additional compliance modules become available, Aserta will apply the same rule-based approach wherever regulatory requirements are sufficiently clear and deterministic.

Review: Where Professional Judgement Matters

Some regulatory requirements are objective.

Where the rules are clear, Aserta applies them. Where the information needs interpretation, Aserta flags it for review.

When Aserta identifies content that cannot be assessed confidently using official regulatory rule sets, it marks the product as Needs Review and records its findings for your team.

This helps focus attention where it is genuinely needed, while allowing routine, rule based checks to be handled automatically.

Aserta supports your compliance process. It does not replace regulatory expertise or certify legal compliance.

Modules for the markets you sell into

UK Food & US FDA (Coming soon)

UK: FIR-style allergen emphasis in English ingredient lists — applied automatically when you use Apply Compliance Updates. US: FDA Big 9 checks; separate Contains: lines are supported and bolding is not required. Both modules re-run when product copy changes.

AU/NZ Beauty & SPF Add-on (Coming soon)

Beauty screens INCI-style composition sections and SUSMP substance keywords on the product page. SPF Add-on (+$5/mo, requires Beauty) adds advisory checks on sunscreen-like pages — SPF on the PDP, AUST L / ARTG references, US vs INCI layout. Cross-border merchants often see Needs Review when US-formatted copy is reused in AU/NZ — that means fix the Shopify listing, not necessarily that the product failed US rules. See FAQ.

California Prop 65 (Coming soon)

Keyword scan against a maintained Prop 65 rule set, optional Safe Harbor warning blocks on the product page, and Needs Review when there is not enough product text to assess. Automatic tags and a metafield trail are added per product — operational guardrails, not a substitute for legal review of your warnings.

Why merchants choose continuous monitoring

Product pages are not static: New products are launched. Variants are added. Marketing copy changes. Ingredients are updated. Product data is imported. Every change creates the possibility that your Shopify product pages no longer reflect your previously reviewed information. Continuous catalogue coverage A few best-selling products are easy to review manually. Large catalogues, seasonal ranges, bundles and long tail products are where inconsistencies are more likely to appear. Aserta continuously monitors your chosen product pages, helping you identify potential compliance issues across your catalogue and not just your best sellers.
Works alongside your existing compliance process:Your regulatory consultant reviews formulations, labels and legal requirements. Aserta helps monitor your live Shopify product pages between those reviews, providing an additional operational layer rather than replacing professional expertise.
Built for operational efficiency: Audit collections in bulk, export PDF audit reports, generate print ready labels from audited product data and when required, you can simply use 'Sweep' to reverse the changes that were applied thus saving time on repetitive catalogue management tasks.
Clarity about what Aserta checks: Aserta analyses Shopify product description HTML only. It does not assess physical packaging, laboratory reports or regulatory certificates. A Needs Review status means a product page deserves attention, not that your product is non-compliant.
