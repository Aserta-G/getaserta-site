How the work is split

Day 1 — the shop

We build a client-transfer Shopify store from a fixed checklist: Grove (Dawn) theme, collections, Home, About, Delivery, Returns, Contact, native policies, navigation. Up to 20 products if you supply them — or empty collections if you will add stock later. Password stays on. Shopify Payments stays off so the transfer is not blocked.

Day 2 — after you own it

You accept the shop, then pick a paid Shopify plan, simply remove the password, and turn Payments on. We can sit with you (Advanced, or as an add-on) while you connect Google Analytics, Search Console, Merchant Center, and a dropship app. We never use our Google login on your shop.

Optional: niche selection

Not a one-day job. You bring at least 10 niche names. You expand to 50+ with our prompt and send the list to us. We apply hard gates. You run Google Keyword Planner (we cannot log into Google Ads). You choose. We do not invent volumes or unpublished wholesale prices.

1. You10 names, then 50+ sent to us. KEEP or DROP.
2. GatesUK median £300+. UK trade supplier. No Alibaba. Online-only multi-brand shops.
3. YouKeyword Planner, one surviving niche at a time.
4. You chooseCHOOSE, HOLD, or DROP. Then we build the shop.

A failed gate stops that idea only. Skip Planner and that niche cannot stay on the shortlist. Trade prices come after a serious shopfront — margin is the final GO, not a research gate.
